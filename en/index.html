01 / Two Faces of Backspin Tokyo

Serious adult lessons by day, an overseas-style ping pong bar by night.

Backspin Tokyo is a cosy, overseas-flavoured space like a British ping pong bar. We care equally about lessons for adults who want to really improve and bar nights where everyone has fun over drinks.

Daytime · Adult & Senior Coaching

Careful, serious table tennis coaching for adults

From active players in their 60s and 70s to complete beginners, we coach movement and form to suit each person. We cover everything from wellness circles to tactical lessons for winning matches.

Nighttime · British Ping Pong Bar

A grown-up ping pong bar of drinks, music and smiles

After work or on weekend nights, come to the ping pong bar with a beer, highball or cocktail in hand. The overseas pub atmosphere is perfect for social clubs and for private parties: year-end, New Year and after-parties.

04 / Instructors

Our instructors

Former corporate-league players and proven coaches help beginners and experienced players improve in a fun and reliable way.

Right-handed shakehand, special rubber on backhand

Her specialty is the short block. With short pips on the backhand, she plays a style that makes the most of special rubbers. If you use special rubbers, or struggle against them, she is the coach for you.
